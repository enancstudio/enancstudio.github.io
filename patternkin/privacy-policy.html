PATTERNKIN Legal

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PATTERNKIN handles when you play it, who else receives some of it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 17, 2026 Applies to: PATTERNKIN for iOS, version 1.0 and later
Terms of Use Support

1. Who we are

PATTERNKIN is made by Enanc Studio, an independent game developer (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, contact us through the PATTERNKIN Support page.

2. The short version

PATTERNKIN has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress and settings stay on your device. We do not run our own servers that receive your data, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Two outside services are involved: Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch an ad for an extra hint, and RevenueCat, only for the optional one-time album unlock.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally so it can remember where you are:

  • Completed levels, best times, and daily challenge progress.
  • Hints used per level.
  • Settings such as sound, music, language, theme, and accessibility options.
  • Whether you have finished the tutorial and whether the album is unlocked.

This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud or device backups, it may be included in those backups under Apple’s terms.

4. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

PATTERNKIN never shows ads on its own. An ad plays only when you tap the option to watch one for an extra hint. Those ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • We request non-personalized ads only. Ads are not chosen based on your past behavior or a profile of you.
  • We do not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the app does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • When an ad is requested, Google may receive information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device model and operating system, an app-specific device identifier, and how you interacted with the ad. Google uses this to deliver the ad, limit how often it is shown, measure it, and prevent fraud.
  • In regions where the law requires it, such as the United Kingdom, Google’s consent message is shown before the first ad. You can change your choices at any time under Settings → “Ad privacy choices”.

Learn more in Google’s How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. The album unlock (Apple and RevenueCat)

PATTERNKIN offers one optional purchase: a one-time album unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details.

To confirm and restore the purchase we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for this app, your App Store transaction and receipt information, and basic device information such as model, operating system, and app version. This lets the app know whether the unlock is active on your devices and lets you restore it after reinstalling. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No precise location, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service in the current version.

If a future version adds a service that handles your data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information reaches Google and RevenueCat only as described above, to show the ads you ask for and to process the purchase. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app. Google and RevenueCat keep the data they receive under their own retention policies. Because we have no account system, we cannot link that data to you by name. If you want us to ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records connected to your app installation, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the UK GDPR or GDPR applies, ads are shown on the basis of the consent you give in Google’s consent message, and purchase records are processed to perform the purchase you made. To exercise any right, contact us as described in section 12.

10. Children

PATTERNKIN is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.

12. Contact

Enanc Studio. To reach us, see the PATTERNKIN Support page.
