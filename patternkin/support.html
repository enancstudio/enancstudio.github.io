Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Contact

Use the developer contact link on the PATTERNKIN page in the App Store. We usually reply within a few days. It helps to include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The app version, shown in the App Store under your purchased apps.
  • Which level or screen, what you expected, and what happened.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

Restoring the album unlock

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy it.
  2. Open PATTERNKIN, go to Settings, and tap “Restore purchase”.

If it still shows as locked, contact us with the date of purchase.

Refunds and billing

Payments are handled by Apple, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices. The album unlock does: restore it as described above.

Ads

PATTERNKIN only shows an ad when you choose to watch one for an extra hint. Ads are non-personalized. If you are in the United Kingdom, you can change your ad consent choices at any time under Settings → “Ad privacy choices”.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. For privacy requests, use the contact route above.
